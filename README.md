 US Healthcare Payer & Provider Analysis

📌 Project Overview

A healthcare consulting and analytics project focused on understanding the US healthcare payer-provider ecosystem, healthcare costs, utilization and value-based care.

The project aims to convert healthcare data into business insights and actionable recommendations.

🎯 Business Objectives

* Understand payer-provider dynamics
* Analyze healthcare cost and utilization patterns
* Identify potential areas of inefficiency
* Examine healthcare performance indicators
* Understand the role of value-based care
* Develop data-driven consulting recommendations

🔍 Key Questions

1. What are the major healthcare cost drivers?
2. How does healthcare utilization vary across populations or providers?
3. Where are potential inefficiencies?
4. What factors may influence healthcare spending?
5. How can value-based care improve healthcare performance?
6. What recommendations could improve cost and outcomes?

🛠️ Tools

* Microsoft Excel
* Power BI
* Data Analysis
* Healthcare Business Analysis

📊 Analysis Areas
